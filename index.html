    Questions, answered.

    Can Fermor move or spend my money?

    No. Connections are read-only. Fermor can see balances and transactions, but it can't make payments or transfers.

    How is my data protected?

    Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We never sell it, and you can delete everything from Settings at any time.

    What does it cost?

    Tracking your accounts, bills and goals is free. Optional paid plans add shared households and longer forecasts.

    Which banks can I connect?

    Most major banks and card providers. If yours isn't supported yet, you can import a statement file instead.
